# SCN010: the estimated scan reaches a policy threshold

The [cost estimate](../estimate.md) says the query would bill at least as many bytes as the policy allows. This is the rule that turns the estimate into a verdict, so a team can say "nothing over 1 TiB runs" and have agents held to it.

| Estimate | Severity |
| --- | --- |
| Low end at or over `block_bytes` (default 1 TiB) | **block** |
| Low end at or over `warn_bytes` (default 100 GiB) | **warn** |
| High end at or over `block_bytes`, at medium or high confidence | **warn** |
| Anything less | not reported |

Both thresholds are set in [`scanisaur.yaml`](../policy.md), in bytes or with a unit (`100GiB`, `500 GB`), and either can be turned `off`. Under capacity (Editions) pricing the thresholds still apply: they count bytes, not dollars.

## Why the low end

The estimate is a range, and the high end is usually far above the bill. It assumes clustering skips no blocks and that a filter whose effect isn't known keeps everything. On the [benchmark](../benchmark.md), seven queries had a high end over 100 GiB, and six of them billed under 60 GB: a week of PyPI downloads for `numpy` estimated up to 732.1 GB and billed 6 GB. Warning on the high end would have flagged all six. The low end is what the query bills at least, so a threshold it reaches is a sure overrun.

A high end that reaches the block threshold still warns: the query may well be cheap, but if it isn't, it is expensive enough to look at first. On the benchmark, that is `pypi-no-partition-filter`, estimated at up to 92.3 TB and billed 564.9 GB. At low confidence even this is skipped, because that high end also assumes no partition is skipped, which is the case of a single-day filter on a table whose partition list isn't known.

## When it stays silent

- **No estimate:** the query can't be analyzed (SCN000), names something that doesn't exist (SCN001), is a blocked write (SCN002), would be rejected for a missing partition filter (SCN003 or SCN004 blocks), or reads a table whose size isn't known. See [when there's no estimate](../estimate.md).
- **An outer `LIMIT 0`,** which reads nothing.
- **A threshold set to `off`,** or the rule set to `off` under `policy.rules`.

## Fix

Read less: filter on the partition column, select only the columns the answer needs, or aggregate in a smaller table first. If the scan is intended, a person, not the agent, raises the threshold in `scanisaur.yaml`.
